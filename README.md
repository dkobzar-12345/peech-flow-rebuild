# Growth Audit — Peech

Five ranked ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Peech already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Peech's own design.

**Live:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/peech-flow-rebuild/
**Straight to the prototype:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/peech-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Make the paywall name what they told you | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Show the plan your loader promises | Your plan | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Make the second chance a saving people can check | Spin wheel → Offer | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | A review from someone like them, while it loads | Loader | CR +5–10% |
| 05 | Say how the 3-day trial ends | Paywall | CR +3–8% |

## Grounding

Every price, rating, review and feature line comes from Peech's own App Store listing, website (getpeech.com) and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Peech.
